# Mission Architect · Görev Mimarı

**▶ Play in your browser:** https://cemyurtlak.github.io/mission-architect/

A 3D browser game for the **NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 – Space Mission Design Game** challenge, made by **Team DeHa Space**.

The player designs, launches and completes a whole crewed mission to the **Moon or Mars**:

1. **Launch site & mission design:** walk to the tower, board the vehicle and use 8 design stations (destination, science goal, instruments, power, communications, crew, rocket & orbit, command) while a live bar tracks budget, mass, power and data.
2. **Failure in orbit:** fix a CO2 problem with items on board (inspired by Apollo 13) and message Earth.
3. **Landing:** fly the lander yourself.
4. **Surface base:** crew health (physical and mental), greenhouse, solar power, exploration, crater, radiation, physics lab and a base farm.
5. **Return to Earth:** cargo limits, the rocket equation, the return window, re-entry corridor, heat shield and parachutes, then rehabilitation.

The game is playable in **English and Turkish** (button on the title screen). It works best in a desktop browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox) with keyboard and mouse.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| W A S D | Move |
| Mouse | Look around (click the screen first) |
| E | Interact |
| Space | Jump / parachutes during re-entry |
| A / D | Keep the heat shield aligned during re-entry |
| B | Knowledge Bank |
| Esc | Pause |

If the game runs slowly, choose **Settings → Low** graphics quality.

## Technology

- Three.js 0.169 (WebGL), bundled with esbuild into a single file: `index.html` contains the whole game and works offline.
- All 3D models, textures and sounds are generated by code; no downloaded models, images or audio.
- Fonts: Exo 2, Rajdhani, Share Tech Mono (SIL Open Font License), embedded.

## NASA data and resources

NASA Planetary Fact Sheets (Moon & Mars), NASA Human Research Program hazards, NASA Veggie, Curiosity RAD radiation measurements, Mars 2020 Perseverance and MOXIE, Artemis I Orion, Apollo 13 and Apollo 15, Kilopower, DSOC, TDRS, CAPSTONE, ISS ECLSS and ARED, post-flight reconditioning reports. Values rounded for game balance are marked "approximate" in the game.

## Use of AI

The game code was written with the help of an AI coding assistant (Claude by Anthropic). The scenarios, testing and decisions were made by the team and its teacher mentor. The 3D scenes are rendered by code, not AI-generated images.
